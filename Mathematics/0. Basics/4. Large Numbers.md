
Adding large numbers:

$$
\begin{array}{r@{}r@{}r@{}r}
  &  & 1 & &  \\
  & 1 & 2 & 9 & 3 \\
+ & 2 & 6 & 1 & 4 \\
\hline
  & 3 & 9 & 0 & 7 \\
\end{array}
$$


Subtracting large numbers:

$$
\begin{array}{r@{}r@{}r@{}r@{}r}
  & 1 & 1 &  &   \\
  & 5 & 2 & 3 & 7 \\
- & 2 & 8 & 9 & 6 \\
\hline
  & 2 & 3 & 4 & 1 \\
\end{array}
$$

Here, since 3 is smaller than 9, we borrow 1 hundred (or 10 tens) from the hundreds column:

$$13-9 = 4$$

And thus we have in the next hundreds column 

$$2-1-8=1-8$$

But again, 1 is smaller than 8, so we borrow 1 thousand (or 10 hundreds) from the thousands column:

$$11-8=3$$

And finally we have $5-1-2=2$, and we get the final answer:

$$2341$$
