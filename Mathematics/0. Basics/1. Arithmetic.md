Addition is commutative; the order in which the terms are placed does not affect the result:

$$2+3=5 \Leftrightarrow 3+2=5$$

But Subtraction is not commutative:

$$3-2=1\neq2-3=-1$$

Addition is associative, meaning that the order in which we choose to add the terms does not affect the outcome:

$$2+3+4=9 \Leftrightarrow (2+3)+4=5+4=9$$
$$2+3+4=9 \Leftrightarrow 2+(3+4)=2+7=9$$

However, Subtraction is not associative:

$$ \begin{align}
(5-3)-2=2-2=0 \\ \neq 5-(3-2)=4
\end{align}
$$


Multiplication, like Addition, is associative:

$$2\cdot5=5\cdot2=10$$

Division, however, like subtraction, is not associative:

$$\frac{\frac{10}{5}}{\frac{2}{1}}=\frac{2}{2}=1$$
$$\neq$$
$$\frac{\frac{10}{1}}{\frac{5}{2}}=\frac{\frac{10}{1}\cdot\frac{2}{5}}{\cancel{\frac{5}{2}}\cdot\cancel{\frac{2}{5}}}=\frac{10}{1}\cdot\frac{2}{5}=\frac{20}{5}=4$$
