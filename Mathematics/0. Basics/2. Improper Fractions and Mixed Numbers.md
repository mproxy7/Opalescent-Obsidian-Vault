
Given an improper fraction:

$$\frac{a}{b}$$

Then

$$\frac{a}{b}=q\cdot\frac{r}{b}$$

Where
$$q \textnormal { is the quotient: } q\cdot\frac{a}{b}$$
$$r \textnormal{ is the remainder}: a \textnormal{ mod } b$$
$$b \textnormal{ is the denominator (unchanged)}$$

Example:

$$\textnormal{Convert } \frac{17}{5} \textnormal{ to a mixed number}$$
Answer:

$$
\begin{align}
r=[17 \textnormal{ mod }5]=2 \\ \Rightarrow 17 = 5 \cdot 3 \\ \textnormal{ with a remainder of 2} \\ \Rightarrow \frac{17}{5}=3\cdot\frac{2}{5}
\end{align}
$$
