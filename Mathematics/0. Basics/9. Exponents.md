
Multiplication is repeated addition:

$$2 \cdot 3 = 2+2+2$$

Consequently, **Exponentiation** is repeated multiplication:

$$2^3 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2$$

Second power $\Rightarrow$ squaring:

$$5^2$$

Third power $\Rightarrow$ cubing:

$$5^3$$


Exponential growth:

$$2^x$$

Implies that each time we increase the exponent, $x$ by one, we are doubling the previous number.

Negative exponents are derived from inverting the term and making the exponent positive:

$$2^{-2} = \frac{1}{2^2} = \frac{1}{4}$$

**Exponent Rules:**
Multiplication between exponents of the same base:
$$x^a \cdot x^b = x^{a+b}$$
Justification:
$$x^2 \cdot x^3 = x \cdot x \cdot x \cdot x \cdot x = x^{2+3} = x^5$$
