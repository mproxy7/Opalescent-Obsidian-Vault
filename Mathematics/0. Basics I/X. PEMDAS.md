
The Order of Arithmetic Operations:

###### 1. Parentheses
###### 2. Exponents
###### 3. Multiplication
###### 4. Division
###### 5. Addition
###### 6. Subtraction

Example exercise:

$$
\begin{align}
&\ \frac{12}{4} + 2(1+3)^2 - 9 \\
= &\ 3 + 2(4)^2 - 9 \\
= &\ 2(16) - 6 \\
= &\ 32 - 6 \\
= &\ 26
\end{align}
$$
