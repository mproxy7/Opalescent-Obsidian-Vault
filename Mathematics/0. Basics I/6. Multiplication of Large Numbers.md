
# First (inefficient) approach:

$$
\begin{array}{r@{}r@{}r@{}r@{}r@{}r}
      &   &   & 3 & 2 & 7 \\[-6pt]
\times &   &   &   & 4 & 6 \\
\hline
      & 1 & 1 & 4 &   &   \\[-6pt] % carry row for first product
      & 1 & 9 & 6 & 2 &   \\[-6pt]
+     & 1 & 3 & 0 & 8 &   \\[-6pt]
\hline
      & 1 & 5 & 0 & 4 & 2 \\
\end{array}
$$

Explanation:
**Step 1: Multiply by the ones digit (6)**

$$327 \times 6$$

- $7 \times 6 = 42 \Rightarrow$ write $2$, carry $4$
- $2 \times 6 = 12$, plus $4$ = $16 \Rightarrow$ write $6$, carry $1$
- $3 \times 6 = 18$, plus $1 = 19 \Rightarrow$ write $19$

So first partial product is $1962$.


**Step 2: Multiply by the tens digit (4, but shifted one place left)**

$$327 \times 40$$

- $7 \times 4 = 28 \Rightarrow$ write $8$, carry $2$
- $2 \times 4 = 8$, plus $2 = 10 \Rightarrow$ write $0$, carry $1$
- $3 \times 4 = 12$, plus $1 = 13 \Rightarrow$ write $13$

Thus the second partial product (shifted) is $1308$

**Step 3: Add them up**

$$1962 + 13080 = 15042$$


# Second, easier approach using the *distributive property*:

**Step 1: Write numbers in expanded form**

$$
\begin{align}
327 = &\ 300 + 20 + 7 \\
46 = &\ 40 + 6
\end{align}
$$

**Step 2: Apply distributive property**

$$
\begin{align}
&\ (300 + 20 + 7)(40 + 6) \\
= &\ (300 \times 40) + (300 \times 6) \\
&\ + (20 \times 40) + (20 \times 6) \\
&\ + (7 \times 40) + (7 \times 6) \\
= &\ 12000 + 1800 + 800 + 120 + 280 + 12 \\
= &\ 15042
\end{align}
$$
