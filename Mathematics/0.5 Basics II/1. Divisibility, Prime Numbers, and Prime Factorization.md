
**Divisibility:** a number is divisible by another number if their quotient is a whole number:

$$\frac{10}{2}=5$$

This means that $2$ and $5$ are factors of $10$.

However, $3$ is not a factor of 10, as it gives us an uneven number, instead we get 3 remainder one:

$$\frac{10}{3}=3r1$$

All numbers have factors, but some numbers are only divisible by itself and by 1. Such numbers are called **prime numbers**.

No even numbers, *except for 2*, can be prime numbers, because they are all divisible by 2. Only odd numbers can be prime numbers.

We can use **Prime Factorization** to express a number using only its prime numbers:

$$
\begin{align}
&\ 20 \\
= &\ 5 \cdot 4 \\
= &\ 5 \cdot 2 \cdot 2
\end{align}
$$

Note: there is only one unique set of prime numbers for any number. This is what's called **The Fundamental Theory of Arithmetic**.
