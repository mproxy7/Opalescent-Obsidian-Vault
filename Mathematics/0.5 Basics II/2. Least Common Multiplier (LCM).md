
We can find the least common multiplier between two numbers using three different methods:

###### 1: Listing Multiples

Example: Find LCM of 4 and 6.

Multiples of 4: 
$$4, 8, 12, 16, 20...$$
Multiples of 6:
$$6, 12, 18, 24$$
This concludes that 12 is the least common multiple.

###### 2: Using Prime Factorization

Example: Find LCM of 12 and 80

$$2 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$$
$$80 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 5$$

Take max powers for each unique base:
$$2^4 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^1 = 16 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 240$$


###### 3: Using GCD (Greatest Common Divisor)

There is a formula:

$$\textnormal{LCM(a,b)} = \frac{a \times b}{\textnormal{GCD(a,b)}}$$

Example: Find LCM of 8 and 12:

$$
\begin{align}
&\ \textnormal{LCM(8,12)} = \frac{12 \times 18}{\textnormal{GCD(12,18)}} \\
\Longleftrightarrow &\ \textnormal{LCM(8,12)} = \frac{96}{4} \\
\Longleftrightarrow &\ \textnormal{LCM(8,12)} = 24 
\end{align}
$$
